🔬 Key Findings

🧠

Spatial Hierarchy vs Flattened Vectors

The 4-stage convolutional hierarchy (32 → 64 → 128 → 256 → 512) preserves 2D spatial locality, capturing fine facial contours, eye corners, and smile curvature. The baseline ANN flattens 4,096 pixels into a 1D vector, discarding spatial relationships.

⚖️

Balanced Sampling & Deduplication

Step 3 caps each emotion class at 5,000 images (up to 35,000 total) to prevent dominant class bias. Step 4 computes MD5 hashes to eliminate exact duplicate images and prevent train/test data leakage.

📉

Regularization & Label Smoothing

Categorical cross-entropy with 0.1 label smoothing prevents overconfident incorrect predictions. Staged dropout (0.10 – 0.40), Batch Normalization, L2 penalty, and EarlyStopping ensure solid generalization.

🎯

CLAHE & Flip TTA Inference

CLAHE (clip limit 2.0, tile 8×8) normalizes ambient lighting across faces. At test and prediction time, flip test-time augmentation (TTA) evaluates both normal and horizontally mirrored images to produce calibrated probabilities.
